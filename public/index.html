FAQ

Questions, answered

Who can apply? +
Any student from the college — coders, designers, researchers, and presenters are all needed.
Can first-year students join? +
Yes. Enthusiasm and skills matter more than year. Apply and show what you can do.
Can I join without a team? +
Absolutely. Apply solo and get matched into a team. Already have a team? Your captain shares a Team ID so the rest join instantly.
How are teams formed? +
After interviews, the core team assigns selected students into balanced six-member teams (with at least one female member, as SIH requires).
What happens after registration? +
You'll see your application status and your interview slot — time and location or meeting link — right in the portal.
How are interviews conducted? +
In person on campus or over a video call — a short conversation about your skills and ideas.
