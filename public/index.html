FAQ

Questions, answered

Who can apply? +
Any student from the college who wants to build for SIH 2026 — coders, designers, researchers, and presenters are all needed.
Can first-year students join? +
Yes. Enthusiasm and skills matter more than year. Apply and show what you can do in the interview.
Can I join without a team? +
Absolutely. Apply solo — after selection you'll be matched into a team. Already have a team? Your captain shares a Team ID so the rest join instantly.
How are teams formed? +
After interviews, the core team assigns selected students into balanced six-member teams (with at least one female member, as SIH requires).
What happens after registration? +
You'll see your application status in the portal, then your interview slot with the time and location or Zoom link.
How are interviews conducted? +
Either in person on campus or over a Zoom link — whatever the core team schedules for you. It's a short conversation about your skills and ideas.
