Questions

Everything you need to know.

Who can apply? +
Any student from the college — developers, designers, researchers, and presenters are all needed.
Can first-year students join? +
Yes. Skills and drive matter more than year. Apply and show what you can do in the interview.
Can I join without a team? +
Absolutely — apply solo and get matched. Already have a team? Your captain shares a Team ID so the rest join instantly.
How are teams formed? +
After interviews, the core team assigns selected students into balanced six-member teams, with at least one female member as SIH requires.
What happens after I register? +
You'll track your status in the portal, then see your interview slot — time and location or meeting link.
How are interviews conducted? +
On campus or over a video call — a short conversation about your skills and ideas.
